# Onboarding Buddy

Turns "they start Monday" into a dated plan built from what the team actually has.

## Inputs
- **Enterprise Search** for the team's existing material: handbooks, architecture notes, runbooks
- **Calendar Management** for who is available in the first fortnight
- `references/config.md` for the role templates and the standing first-week items

## Procedure
1. **Establish** the start date, the role and the team. Missing any of these, ask once and stop.
2. **Find the real material.** Search for what this team has written: onboarding notes, decision
   records, runbooks, recent all-hands. Record what exists and, importantly, what does not.
3. **Find the people** the role should meet, evidenced by who owns the systems and documents the
   role will touch. Not an org chart walk.
4. **Build the plan day by day** across ten working days, each day carrying at most one meeting
   and one reading item. A plan with six meetings on day one is a plan nobody follows.
5. **Propose calendar holds.** Do not create them.
6. **List the gaps.** If there is no runbook for a system the role owns, that is the most useful
   output in the whole plan.

## Output
Day-by-day table:

| Day | Date | Meet | Why them | Read | Source |

Then: material that exists, material that is missing, and the proposed holds for the user to
approve.

## Definition of done
Ten working days planned, every reading item linked, every person justified by what they own, and
the missing material named.

**A short plan with real links beats a full one with placeholders.** If the team has written
little, say so: the gap list becomes the onboarding plan's most valuable section.

## When there is no data
- No team material found: say so clearly and build the plan from people only, marking every
  reading slot as MATERIAL NEEDED.
- Start date unknown: do not assume next Monday.

## Boundaries
- **Never create calendar events or send invitations.** Propose holds for approval.
- Never include anything about the joiner's compensation, background or personal details.
- Never surface documents the joiner will not have permission to open once they start: note them
  as access needed instead.
- Draft only.

## Self-check
- Is every person justified by ownership, not hierarchy?
- Is every reading item a real link?
- Did I name what is missing rather than papering over it?

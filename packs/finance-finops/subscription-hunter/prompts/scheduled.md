# Scheduled prompt

**Recommended cadence: monthly.** Costs one of the user's 25 scheduled prompts.

Weekly is tempting and wrong here: renewal dates move monthly at most, and a weekly run that
reports the same unchanged list four times a month is how a reader learns to ignore it.

> On the first Monday of each month, use the subscription-hunter skill. Report only what CHANGED
> since the last run: new subscriptions found, renewals now inside 30 days, and anything that
> stopped charging. Draft the cancellation replies for the Likely waste rows and leave them
> unsent. If nothing changed, reply with one line saying so.

That last sentence is the point of the schedule. Silence is the success state.

# Sample input: a month of Northwind Energy Projects mail

Fictional. Twelve messages reaching the mailbox of a project controls lead.

| # | From | Subject | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | billing@driftplan.io | Your DriftPlan receipt, 48 seats | Monthly, arrives on the 3rd, nine months running |
| 2 | no-reply@driftplan.io | Seats updated: 48 to 62 | Seat creep nobody mentioned |
| 3 | accounts@vectorgantt.com | Invoice VG-88214, annual plan | Annual, renews 14 Jan, auto-renew stated |
| 4 | hello@tracewell.app | Your trial ends in 3 days | Converts automatically unless cancelled |
| 5 | billing@tracewell.app | Payment successful, Tracewell Pro | The trial converted. Nobody noticed |
| 6 | noreply@siteledger.co | Receipt, SiteLedger Team | Last charge 14 months ago, then silence |
| 7 | contracts@northfield-eng.com | Countersigned: Master Services Agreement | 90-day notice period, buried in clause 11 |
| 8 | billing@vectorgantt.com | Your plan renews in 30 days | The only renewal warning in the set |
| 9 | billing@driftplan.io | Your DriftPlan receipt, 62 seats | The seat increase is now billing |
| 10 | shop@toolhaus.example | Order confirmation, site tablet | One-off. Must NOT appear as a subscription |
| 11 | billing@vectorgantt.com | Receipt, annual plan | Duplicate evidence for row 3, not a second row |
| 12 | team@driftplan.io | Product update, November | Not a billing message. Must be ignored |

**What makes this a fair test:** a trial that converted silently, seat creep, a vendor that went
quiet, a notice period hidden in a contract rather than an email, a one-off purchase that must not
be misread as recurring, and duplicate evidence that must collapse into one row.

# Leadership Update config
- `max_words: 400`
- `cadence: weekly`

## Structure
1. Where we are
2. What changed since last update
3. What is at risk
4. What I need from you

## Escalation bar
Anything meeting one of these appears in the update, unsoftened.
- a committed date has moved
- a dependency is blocked for more than 5 working days
- spend is tracking above plan
- a risk previously rated low has risen

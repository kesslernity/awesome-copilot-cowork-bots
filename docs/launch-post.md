# Launch material

## LinkedIn, 178 words

Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork can send the email, post in Teams and run the job while you are not
watching. Most of what is written about it stops at "look what it can do".

The harder question is what you let it do unsupervised, and the honest answer for most teams is:
less than the demo suggests, and more than they currently allow.

I have put 23 Cowork bots in a public repo. Every one ships read-and-prepare first. Sending,
posting and deleting sit behind an approval you give each time, and several refuse categories of
action outright: the calendar one never moves a meeting, the 1:1 one never characterises a person.

The designs are adapted from patterns proven in Grok Bot. Four of its seven published roles do not
port to Cowork at all, because they need a terminal or an ad platform, and the repo says which and
why rather than shipping versions that half work.

Nine more were cut because Microsoft already ships them. A catalogue is more useful when it tells
you what it is not.

Free, MIT, link in the first comment.
